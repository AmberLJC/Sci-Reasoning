Kallenberg’s modern probability consolidates the needed machinery—Markov/transition kernels, regular conditional probabilities, and stochastic processes—making kernels the natural vehicle for compositional causal specification.

Together these works converge to a kernel-based axiomatization that unifies interventions, cycles, latents, and stochastic processes.
