### 🏗️ Foundation

**TabDDPM: Modelling Tabular Data with Diffusion Models** (2023)
- *Authors:* Kotelnikov et al.
- *Direct Connection:* TabWak relies on the TabDDPM formulation where each table row is synthesized from an independently seeded Gaussian latent, enabling watermark embedding by controlling those per-row seeds without modifying the diffusion backbone.

**STaSy: Score-based Tabular Data Synthesis** (2021)
- *Authors:* Kim et al.
- *Direct Connection:* By establishing score-based diffusion for mixed-type tabular data with Gaussian noise initialization per instance, STaSy provides the latent-noise mechanism that TabWak exploits to encode signatures in the sampling noise for each row.

**Watermarking Relational Databases** (2002)
- *Authors:* Agrawal and Kiernan
- *Direct Connection:* Classic tuple-level database watermarking motivated TabWak’s row-wise detectability and permutation-tolerant design, inspiring the use of key-driven selection/shuffling so watermarks persist under row reordering and common table edits.

### 💡 Inspiration

**Tree-Ring Watermarks: Invisible Watermarks for Diffusion Models** (2023)
- *Authors:* Wen et al.
- *Direct Connection:* Tree-Ring showed that robust diffusion watermarks can be embedded by manipulating the initial Gaussian noise rather than model weights, directly motivating TabWak’s choice to watermark tabular diffusion by controlling the latent noise via seeded randomness.

- *Authors:* Zhao et al.
- *Direct Connection:* Model-embedded diffusion watermarks like Stable Signature require access and retraining, highlighting a practical gap that TabWak addresses by embedding watermarks purely through seed control without modifying tabular diffusion models.

## Synthesis: How Prior Work Led to This Paper

Score-based and denoising diffusion approaches for tabular data established that each synthetic row is generated from an independently sampled Gaussian latent, with TabDDPM codifying a widely used backbone and STaSy demonstrating effective handling of mixed-type columns via per-instance noise initialization. Concurrently, diffusion watermarking for images revealed a crucial lever: manipulating the initial noise can embed robust, model-agnostic signatures, as Tree-Ring Watermarks showed by encoding signals directly in the Gaussian latent rather than retraining the generator. In parallel, LLM watermarking introduced secret-keyed, position-dependent hashing to steer generation toward detectable distributions, with statistical detection that remains resilient to post-editing. Longstanding database watermarking further showed how key-driven tuple selection and permutation-tolerant embedding can enable row-wise traceability despite row reordering and common table operations. By contrast, model-embedded diffusion watermarks (e.g., Stable Signature) underscored deployment limitations when generator access or retraining is required. Together, these works suggested a natural opportunity: harness the per-row Gaussian latent in tabular diffusion as the watermark carrier, but encode the key through the positional structure of seeds rather than content perturbations, enabling row-level detectability at scale. Building on LLM-style keyed hashing, seed self-cloning and shuffling realize position-aware signaling, while a valid-bit focus on distribution tails analogizes green-list selection to boost robustness against noisy post-edits—all without modifying the diffusion backbone.
