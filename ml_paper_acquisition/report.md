### Data Schema

Each paper record includes:
- `title`: Paper title
- `authors`: Comma-separated author list
- `abstract`: Paper abstract (truncated to 1000 chars)
- `keywords`: Paper keywords
- `conference`: ICLR, ICML, or NeurIPS
- `year`: 2023, 2024, or 2025
- `presentation_type`: oral, spotlight, or poster
- `openreview_id`: Unique OpenReview identifier
- `forum_id`: OpenReview forum ID

## Methodology

### Data Collection

1. **Primary Source**: OpenReview API v2
2. **Supplementary Source**: Paper Copilot statistics for ICLR 2023 (API v1)
3. **Extraction Dates**: December 29, 2025 (2023-2024) and December 31, 2025 (2025)

### Presentation Type Classification

Papers were classified based on:
1. The `venue` field in OpenReview (most reliable)
2. The `venueid` field as a fallback

### Limitations

- ICLR 2023 uses OpenReview API v1; statistics included from Paper Copilot
- Some conferences may have additional tracks (e.g., Datasets and Benchmarks) not included

## Key Insights

### 1. Rapid Growth in ML Research
- Total papers increased **85%** from 2023 to 2025
- ICLR showed the most dramatic growth (+135%)

### 2. Consistent Selectivity
- High-impact paper rate remains stable at ~13.5%
- Oral presentations are highly selective (3.5% overall)

### 3. Conference Differentiation
- **ICLR**: Highest proportion of high-impact papers (18.2%)
- **NeurIPS**: Largest volume, most spotlights
- **ICML**: Balanced distribution, introduced spotlights in 2024

### 4. Spotlight Category Expansion
- Spotlights now outnumber orals ~3:1
- NeurIPS 2025 had 687 spotlights (highest ever)
